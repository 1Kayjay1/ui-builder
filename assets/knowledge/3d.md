# 3D

3D earns its cost when it communicates product/spatial information, enables meaningful direct manipulation, or carries a narrative/identity not efficiently achieved in 2D. Require graceful fallback, frame/battery/loading budgets, and equivalent semantic access. Three.js is the broad core; R3F is optional in React; glTF/GLB is preferred interchange. Provide a 2D semantic twin for interactive 3D information.
