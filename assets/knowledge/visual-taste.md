# Visual taste

Analyze hierarchy, rhythm, density, grouping, alignment, typography, color roles, surfaces, navigation, motion, feedback, distinctiveness, restraint, and likely failure mode. Replace vague words such as “premium” with concrete observations and tradeoffs.

Use: OBSERVATION → PRINCIPLE → TRANSFERABILITY → DO NOT COPY. Ask what job each visual motif performs. If the only reason is “it looks modern,” reconsider it. Excess cards, pills, purple gradients, glass, glow, centered hero copy, decorative blobs, huge whitespace, icon-square grids, fake charts, random radii, and ubiquitous animation are not banned; they require a job.
