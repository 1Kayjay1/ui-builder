# Performance

Track product performance and effect cost. Core Web Vitals field targets at p75: LCP ≤2.5s, INP ≤200ms, CLS ≤0.1. Project-specific budgets may cover JS, fonts, images, DOM size, long tasks, canvas resolution, particle counts, and hidden animation. Watch large blur/backdrop areas, oversized composited layers, layout-triggering animation, high-DPR fullscreen canvas, and offscreen scenes still rendering.
